Our Story

Considered goods, from Nairobi to your door.

LUXE MARKET started in 2021 as a two-person operation sourcing quality basics for friends and family. Today we work with over 40 local and international brands to bring premium, honestly-priced products to shoppers across Kenya.

Our Mission

To make premium quality accessible — no markup games, no filler products, just goods worth owning.

Our Vision

To become East Africa's most trusted destination for considered, long-lasting products across every category.

Why Shop With Us

Curated brands, fast Nairobi delivery, secure M-Pesa checkout, and a 30-day no-questions returns policy.
